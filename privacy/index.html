XC Shots · privacy

What the app keeps, and why.

XC Shots is the filming app XC Media gives its clients. It holds your shot list, takes the clips you choose to send, and tells you when each one has arrived. XC Media is a sole trader business in Australia. Questions or a deletion request go to info@xcmedia.co.

What it collects

  • Your studio code and the team name you tap, so the app knows which business and which person is filming.
  • The clips and photos you choose to send. The app only sees the ones you pick. It never reads the rest of your camera roll.
  • What happens to your shots: ticks, notes, the time each clip arrived and its size, so you and XC can see nothing went missing.
  • If your business has an owner login, the owner PIN. On iPhone it is kept in the phone's own Keychain and unlocked with Face ID.

The app has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. It doesn't collect your location or contacts.

Where it goes

  • Google: clips are stored in XC Media's Google Drive, and the shot list and its history in XC Media's Google account.
  • AssemblyAI: the sound of each clip is transcribed so XC can match it to the script. Transcripts stay with XC Media.
  • WhatsApp, through Chakra: only if your business has switched on WhatsApp updates, the app's messages go to the number you gave XC.

Nothing is sold, rented or shared for marketing. The people who see your footage are XC and the editor cutting your reels.

How long it's kept

For as long as XC Media works with your business, plus the time needed to deliver the last reels. After that, ask and it's deleted. Reels already posted to your own accounts are yours.

Your choices

  • You decide which clips to send. Nothing uploads by itself.
  • Notifications are optional and can be turned off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • You can ask what's held about you, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be deleted, at info@xcmedia.co.
