Before every shot — the five rules

1

Wipe the lens on your shirt.

Ten seconds. Fixes more "blurry" footage than anything else on this page.

2

Find the window.

Daylight from a window beats every overhead light and every filter. Light on the subject, not behind it.

3

Hold it upright. Both hands.

Portrait, elbows against your body. If you can lean the phone on something, do.

4

Get closer than feels normal.

Then closer again. Never zoom — zoom makes it blurry. Move your feet.

5

Count it out. Then stop.

Every shot says how long. Count in your head, stop on the number. Long is fine; short can't be fixed.

Phone setup — once, then forget it

iPhone

  • Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K at 30 fps
  • Settings → Camera → Grid ON (helps you keep things straight)
  • In the camera: tap the subject, then hold until "AE/AF LOCK" — light stops jumping

Android

  • Camera → Settings → Video → 4K (UHD) 30 fps
  • Camera → Settings → Grid lines ON
  • Tap the subject, then the lock icon so exposure stays put

Uploading

  • Use the Google Drive app, not the browser — it keeps uploading in the background, even with the phone locked
  • Clips disappear from the folder within a minute — that's XC receiving them, not a failure
  • Upload first, then tick the shot
